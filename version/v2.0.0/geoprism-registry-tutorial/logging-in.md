# Logging in

## With a Geoprism Registry account

1.  On the Geoprism Registry login page, type your username and password.

    <figure><img src="https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/nyz9L2nR22zu_gPQQooghRC9Fcm_6xAyx3sMtGZzP6vzvxDDzCls0UUZogMb9qf1wixR-5o1MW3DJpORQ3xln2KLh64W2aJIbfSKP46LqYH2MQhu-9ADcQ53wF-OA8Ok-xPWGlN8IGUR6tb71xtfq9QRdbjGgzXZbF5WdM0xEl3p8vkyTsGXfOEuVg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  Click **Login**.

If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot password?**. See [resetting-password.md](user-management/resetting-password.md "mention").

## With an external account

If your Geoprism Registry is connected to an external system that supports OAuth, such as DHIS2, the login page also shows **Log in with** below the login form. Click it and choose the system, then sign in with your account on that system. Your Geoprism Registry account must be set up for OAuth first. See [adding-users.md](user-management/adding-users.md "mention").
