# Introduction

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2022-11-01 143354.jpg" alt="" width="331"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Geoprism Registry is an online platform for hosting, maintaining and sharing geographic data over time. It manages lists of geographic objects (Geo-Objects), such as provinces, districts or health facilities, together with their hierarchies, geometries and attributes. Every change is tracked over time, so you can see what the data looked like on any date.

Geoprism Registry can act as a common geo-registry (CGR): a single, reliable source of geographic data that any information system can use. See [gpr-as-a-cgr.md](gpr-as-a-cgr.md "mention") for background on the CGR concept.

## What you can do with Geoprism Registry

* **Model your data.** Define Geo-Object Types and the hierarchies that link them, classify Geo-Objects with Concepts, and add non-geospatial data with Business Types. See [Configure](configure/README.md).
* **Load and maintain data.** Import spreadsheets, shapefiles and relationships, review changes through change requests, record historical events such as mergers and splits, and roll back imports if needed. See [Curate](curate/README.md).
* **Explore and share data.** View Geo-Objects on a map, publish lists and spatial data for a date or period, and publish Spatial Knowledge Graphs for other systems. See [Explore](explore/README.md).
* **Connect other systems.** Share data with external systems such as Apache Jena and FHIR, or use the APIs. See [External system integration](external-system-integration/README.md).
