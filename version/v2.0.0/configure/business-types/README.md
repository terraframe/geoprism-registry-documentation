# Business Types

A **Business Type** defines a kind of data that isn't geospatial, such as staff records, programs or survey results. Like a Geo-Object Type, it has attributes, and each record of that type is a **business object**. Business objects can be related to each other, or to Geo-Objects, through Business Edge Types.

The **Business Types** page has two sections, which you choose at the top of the list:

* **Business Types**. See [adding-a-business-type.md](adding-a-business-type.md "mention"), [editing-a-business-type.md](editing-a-business-type.md "mention"), [managing-attributes-of-a-business-type.md](managing-attributes-of-a-business-type.md "mention") and [delete-a-business-type.md](delete-a-business-type.md "mention").
* **Business Edge Types**. See [business-edge-types.md](business-edge-types.md "mention").

To load business objects, use [Import Business Data](../../curate/import-business-data.md). To load the relationships between them, use [Import Edge Data](../../curate/import-edge-data.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
If you don't see **Business Types** in the sidebar, business data may not be enabled for your installation.
{% endhint %}
