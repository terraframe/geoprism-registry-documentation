# Version 2.0.0

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (111).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Geoprism Registry is certified as a Digital Public Good (DPG) of the DPG Alliance.

Geoprism Registry is an open-source platform for managing geographic data and the data related to it over time. Organizations use it to maintain an authoritative registry of geographic objects, such as administrative areas and health facilities, with their hierarchies, geometries and history, and to share that data with other systems.

## Get started

* **New to Geoprism Registry?** Start with the [Introduction](introduction.md).
* **Setting up a server?** See [Deployment and setup](deployment-and-setup/README.md).
* **Logging in or managing users?** See [Access and Permissions](geoprism-registry-tutorial/README.md).

## Using Geoprism Registry

* [Configure](configure/README.md): define the structure of your data, including Geo-Object Types, hierarchies, Concepts and Business Types.
* [Curate](curate/README.md): load, correct and maintain your data over time.
* [Explore](explore/README.md): view your data on a map and in lists, and publish it for other systems.
* [External system integration](external-system-integration/README.md): connect Geoprism Registry to other systems.
