Geoprism Registry is certified as a Digital Public Good (DPG) of the DPG Alliance.

Welcome to the Geoprism Registry (GPR) documentation. GPR is a translation layer that helps organizations bring time-aware data into AI while maintaining high quality, common geographies, common IDs, and provenance. This guide explains how to set up the platform, configure and curate your geographic data, and publish it for use by AI systems and other applications.

## Get started

* **New to Geoprism Registry?** Start with the [Introduction](introduction.md).
* **Setting up a server?** See [Deployment and setup](deployment-and-setup/).
* **Logging in or managing users?** See [Access and Permissions](geoprism-registry-tutorial/).

## Using Geoprism Registry

* [Configure](configure/): define the structure of your data, including Geo-Object Types, hierarchies, Concepts and Business Types.
* [Curate](curate/): load, correct and maintain your data over time.
* [Explore](explore/): view your data on a map and in lists, and publish it for other systems.
* [External system integration](external-system-integration/): connect Geoprism Registry to other systems.
